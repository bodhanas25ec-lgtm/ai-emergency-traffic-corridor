# AI Emergency Traffic Corridor

## 1. Project Overview
This project explores how artificial intelligence can help reduce delays faced by emergency vehicles due to traffic congestion and red signals.

## 2. Problem Statement
Emergency vehicles such as ambulances and fire engines lose critical response time because traffic congestion along their routes is not identified early and traffic signals are not cleared in time.

## 3. Project Objective
- Identify traffic congestion along emergency routes.
- Analyse traffic data to detect possible delays.
- Explore congestion-aware route suggestions.
- Support traffic signal coordination under authorized human supervision.
- Reduce emergency vehicle waiting time while maintaining road safety.

## 4. Proposed Approach
1. Collect permitted traffic data.
2. Analyse congestion and junction delays.
3. Identify possible emergency route delays.
4. Explore traffic signal coordination with authorized human oversight.
5. Evaluate accuracy, journey time and safety.
